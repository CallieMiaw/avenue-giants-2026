The Drive Up & The Hike

Sunday, September 6

Straight up 101 to Humboldt County, a quick bite in town, then into the redwoods for the two must-see groves and the real hike of the trip. Daylight stays outdoors today, the hotel doesn't see you until after dark.

Depart Santa Clara

6:30–7:00am

Direct via 101, ~5.5–6 hrs to Garberville/Miranda. Longer haul than leaving from SF, worth the earlier start.

Arrive Garberville / Miranda & Lunch

~12:00–12:30pm arrive 12:30–1:15pm lunch

The Woodrose Cafe — organic, fresh, great sit-down lunch. Or Eel River Cafe for 1950s-diner charm and hearty classics. Grab-and-go instead: Il Forno Bakery for a breakfast sandwich and pastry to eat in the car.

Founders Grove + Rockefeller Loop

1:15–2:15pm Easy · 0.5mi each

The two essential short stops. Founders Grove holds the Dyerville Giant, a 362-ft fallen redwood you can walk the full length of. Rockefeller Loop has some of the tallest trees on Earth.

Bull Creek Flats Trail

2:15–5:15pm Moderate · Half-day

The real hike. Follows Bull Creek upstream through the heart of the old-growth flat, past the Giant Tree. Pick your own turnaround point.

🚗 ~45min–1hr further north toward Loleta

Sunset at Table Bluff

~6:15–7:45pm Coastal overlook

10 minutes from the hotel. Last light of the day, ocean views over the bluff, the payoff for staying out until dark instead of heading in early.

🚗 Short drive, arrive after dark

Check In, Dinner & Poker

~8:00pm Chill night

Rivers Edge Grill & Bar for a proper sit-down dinner with valley views, or Thirsty Bear Lounge for something more casual, pub food, cocktails, live music Fri/Sat. Then the tables. No rush, no agenda after this.

Roadside Stops & Home

Monday, September 7 · Labor Day

Breakfast next door in Ferndale, then a string of classic redwood-country roadside oddities heading south, a real lunch stop, and a race against Labor Day traffic to get home while there's still light for pickleball.

Slow Morning

Wake up whenever

No alarm needed after a late poker night. Breakfast waits for Ferndale, just next door.

🚗 ~10–15 min, right next door

Breakfast & Ferndale

9:30–10:45am Victorian village

Poppa Joe's — the local institution, bottomless $2 coffee, huge portions, cash only. It even has a poker room in back, fitting given last night. For specialty coffee instead, Mind's Eye Manufactory & Coffee Lounge has third-wave espresso and pour-over. Wander Main Street's Victorian storefronts after.

🚗 ~1hr south, back onto 101

Shrine Drive-Thru Tree

~11:45am–12:00pm Myers Flat

Right on the Avenue. Drive your own car through a genuine tunnel carved into a fire-hollowed redwood, a classic bit of roadside Americana since before the highway was even paved.

🚗 ~45 min further south

Confusion Hill

~12:45–1:15pm Piercy

Gravity-defying "tilt house" optical illusions, plus a switchback mountain train if you want to extend the stop. Genuinely fun, weird, photogenic. Chandelier Tree in Leggett is close by too, but reviews note the access road is bumpy and larger vehicles often don't fit through, treat it as an optional bonus, not a must.

🚗 ~1hr south

Lunch — Loose Caboose Cafe

~2:00–2:45pm Closes 3pm, don't dawdle

Willits. Sandwiches, pizza, soups, salads, coffee, dog-friendly covered patio. A real sit-down lunch before the long final stretch.

🚗 ~1–1.5 hrs to Healdsburg

Pastry Stop — Quail & Condor

~4:00–4:30pm Call ahead, morning bakes can sell out

Healdsburg Ave. One review put it bluntly: "the croissant lamination and quality is A+++, cannot distinguish them from award-winning croissants in Paris." If they're out, Costeaux French Bakery (417 Healdsburg Ave) is the reliable backup, a local institution since 1923.

🚗 ~2–2.5 hrs remaining south

Arrive Santa Clara

Est. ~6:45–7:45pm Labor Day traffic risk

Real range here, not a firm number, Labor Day Monday evening is one of the heaviest southbound travel windows of the year on 101/Bay Area approaches. Earlier stops = better odds of the early end of this window.

🎾 If it's still bright

Pickleball with Brian & Ari

Bonus, not guaranteed Sunset ~7:20–7:30pm

Only really works if you land on the earlier end of the arrival window. Dinner can wait until after, grab something nearby once you're off the court rather than adding another stop and eating into daylight.

Good to Know

The Drive

~5.5–6 hrs each way from Santa Clara direct via 101, a bit longer than starting from SF. The redwoods sit right along the way, no backtracking either direction.

Labor Day Traffic

Monday evening southbound is one of the year's heaviest travel windows. Treat the return-time estimate as a range, not a promise, and keep stops tight if pickleball matters to you.

Redwood National Park

Another 2 hrs north of Garberville, tempting since you're already up there, but it deserves its own dedicated trip (Fern Canyon, Tall Trees Grove) rather than a rushed bolt-on.

Pack Layers

Temperatures range 55–85°F depending on sun/shade and time of day. Mornings under the canopy run cool.
